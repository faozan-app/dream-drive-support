Dream Drive Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Dream Drive respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

Dream Drive does not require users to create an account or sign in. The app does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or messages.

Local Game Data

Game progress, selected vehicle, unlocked purchases, and related game settings may be stored locally on your device so that your progress can be restored when you reopen the app.

In-App Purchases

Dream Drive offers an optional one-time Non-Consumable purchase to unlock the motorcycle. Purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. Dream Drive does not receive or store your payment card details.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple services for App Store purchases and purchase restoration. These services may process information according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

Dream Drive does not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game data stored locally on your device can generally be removed by deleting the app. Purchase records are managed by Apple and may be restored using Restore Purchases.

Children's Privacy

Dream Drive does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
